# Provisional role and access-control matrix

## Status and source basis

This is a proposal for review, not a policy approved for a specific deployment
or an authorization to access real learner data. Supabase Auth password
sign-in and recovery are implemented in source but no project is configured.
The prototype has no workspace database, PostgreSQL grants, RLS, or backend
authorization. Demo account selection and client-side navigation remain
simulation. Every conditional or pending entry requires deployment review and
trusted implementation before it can grant access. No matrix entry is
currently an approved operational permission.

The demo System Administrator's client-side navigation is limited to a
data-free System Administration status page. This navigation preset does not
approve or grant any entity permission in this proposal.

The approved target distinguishes account, platform, and workspace scopes.
Authentication does not create membership. Workspace membership and
role/assignment are separate, scoped grants. `Platform Operator` is a
platform-level role and does not imply membership or routine access to learner
records. A `Workspace Owner` and authorized `Workspace Administrator` may
manage their own workspace only within approved boundaries; neither receives
platform authority. The legacy `Designated System Administrator` concept is
technical/provider administration, not a workspace role and not a grant to
learner or guidance data.

This update reconciles the conceptual entities in
[DATA-MODEL-ERD.md](./DATA-MODEL-ERD.md) with their fields and unresolved
details in [DATA-DICTIONARY.md](./DATA-DICTIONARY.md), the existing provisional
role boundaries, and the relevant project requirements. It does not create
school policy, legal interpretation, or a permission architecture.

## Decision legend

Each entity cell refers to a profile defined below. Every profile specifies
Read, Create, Update, Delete, Correct, and Export:

- **C — Conditional:** a proposed, narrow permission only if the deployment institution approves the
  stated purpose, role, and scope. It is not active now.
- **P — Pending deployment review:** no permission is proposed until the relevant
  workflow or boundary is decided. It is not active now.
- **D — Denied:** denied by this draft/default, including routine access or
  destructive changes to historical records.

There are no **Allowed** entries because no permissions have been approved.
For all profiles, `Correct` means a separately authorized, attributable change
to an existing record; it is not implied by `Update`. `Export` is separate
from viewing. A profile marked `—` is not used in that matrix cell.

## Provisional roles and scope

- **Platform Operator:** manage platform registration invitations,
  workspace-creation grants, and platform configuration only. Bootstrap this
  privilege out of band. Do not grant routine workspace learner-record access.
- **Workspace Owner:** manage membership, configuration, roles, assignments,
  and approved operations within the owned workspace. Ownership does not
  grant platform authority or unrestricted confidential guidance access.
- **Workspace Administrator:** manage ordinary membership, roles,
  assignments, and approved structure only within the assigned workspace and
  only within delegated authority. Cannot self-promote to Owner or Platform
  Operator.
- **Workspace membership:** pending join requests grant no access. Users can
  belong to multiple workspaces with different roles; the workspace context
  must be enforced by PostgreSQL constraints, grants, and RLS.

- **Teacher:** any conditional class access is limited to currently approved
  teaching assignments and their subject offerings/classes. The role alone
  grants no roster, section, or case scope.
- **Adviser:** conditional learner access is limited to the currently
  approved advisory assignment. Basic profile changes and follow-up are
  limited to the approved adviser workflow.
- **Guidance Counselor:** access is limited to an approved guidance function
  and assigned referral/case scope. Guidance records remain a separately
  restricted domain; a general support-case assignment does not by itself
  grant guidance-record access.
- **Parent/Guardian:** future access is conditional on verified identity and
  learner relationship, and limited to information and communications
  explicitly approved for that relationship. The ERD does not yet include a
  guardian-link entity.
- **Designated System Administrator:** legacy technical/account
  administration concept; it does not grant routine learner, academic,
  attendance, warning, support-note, or guidance-content access. Do not
  conflate it with Platform Operator, Workspace Owner, or Workspace
  Administrator.
- **Matrix coverage:** the entity table below is the older monitoring-role
  proposal and does not yet enumerate the full Workspace Owner and Workspace
  Administrator management actions. Do not infer an unlisted grant; define
  each workspace management action explicitly and test it with RLS before
  enabling it.

A user may have multiple roles and multiple assignment records. Evaluate each
role, assignment, entity, action, and record scope independently. Do not use
“highest role wins,” infer access from a role label, or let one assignment
expand another assignment's scope. Record the acting identity and applicable
authorization context for consequential changes. Assignment authority,
effective dates, and role-combination rules still need deployment decisions.

### Entity-matrix interpretation notes

- `ATTENDANCE_CHANGE`, `ASSESSMENT_SCORE_CHANGE`, `WARNING_REVIEW_EVENT`, and
  `AUDIT_EVENT` are history/event records. Their matrix profiles do not permit
  editing, deleting, or correcting the history row itself.
- `ASSESSMENT` uses `A`, not `E`, because the ERD has no assessment-change
  history. Corrections to assessment definitions are pending until an
  attributable history process is approved.
- `GUIDANCE_CASE_ASSIGNMENT` is an assignment record, not an authorization
  policy. A separate approval must define whether and how it grants case
  access. Neither technical administration nor general support access grants
  access to `GUIDANCE_RECORD`.
- The ERD's `USER_PROFILE`, `ROLE`, and `USER_ROLE` are proposed future
  authorization-supporting records, not evidence of live accounts or roles.
- Parent access is future-only. `PARENT` applies solely to a verified linked
  learner and an approved information subset; it does not grant internal
  warnings, support notes, guidance data, staff records, or exports.
- No matrix profile authorizes deletion of historical educational, warning,
  support, guidance, announcement-read, or audit history. Archival and
  retention procedures require separate approval.

## Cross-cutting enforcement boundaries

1. Apply authorization at the trusted data layer for each read and write;
   hidden controls or client-side role state are not enforcement.
2. Resolve record scope from approved assignments and historical enrollment
   context. A current section must not silently replace the section that
   applied when an attendance or score record was created.
3. Keep guidance records, content, and any guidance-only audit detail
   separate from general teacher, administrator, parent, and support views.
4. An announcement's `audience_type` or `section_id` is targeting metadata,
   not proof that a user is authorized to read it.
5. Apply the same restrictions to derived totals, print previews, downloads,
   exports, and any attachments introduced later.
6. Preserve historical records. Corrections should record actor, before/after
   values, and time where the ERD provides a history entity; do not casually
   update or delete history rows.
7. Do not infer access from a role alone. Multiple roles, assignments, and
   scopes must be checked independently for every action.
8. Do not add a guardian link, content field, role, permission, warning rule,
   or deployment-specific procedure unless it is approved and documented.

## Attendance-first allow/deny test plan

These are required future backend/RLS checks, not tests of the current demo.
No scenario below makes a permission active; conditional allows require
documented deployment institution approval and server-side implementation.

| Case | Expected backend result |
|---|---|
| Anonymous request reads or writes a session, entry, or change | Deny |
| Authenticated Teacher with a current approved assignment for the selected offering and approved attendance permission reads roster and records that section | Allow only within that offering and applicable enrollment scope |
| Teacher with no assignment, an inactive/revoked assignment, or an assignment for another offering/section | Deny, including direct API requests |
| Authenticated Adviser with the current assigned advisory section | Attendance access remains pending the deployment institution confirmation; if approved, allow only that section |
| Adviser without that assignment or with a different section assignment | Deny |
| Guidance Counselor requests general session, entry, or correction-table access | Deny; any minimum referral context requires a separate approved data path and policy |
| Disabled Parent/Guardian requests attendance data | Deny |
| Designated System Administrator requests attendance records or corrections | Deny |
| Workspace Owner/Administrator attempts an action outside their workspace or delegated scope | Deny |
| Any actor submits an enrollment from another section, an enrollment outside its effective dates, or an unknown session | Reject atomically; create no partial batch |
| Duplicate entry for the same session/enrollment, including concurrent submissions | Reject or idempotently avoid duplicate according to the reviewed transaction design |
| User attempts to grant themselves a role or assignment | Deny |
| Authorized correction | Require actor identity and append previous/new status and timestamp; do not overwrite or delete history |

Review the Teacher and Adviser scenarios after authorized deployment representatives decide who may record,
whether advisers may correct records entered by another staff member, and how
assignment effective dates are approved and revoked.

## Decisions requiring deployment institution approval

- Which staff positions map to Teacher, Adviser, Guidance Counselor, and
  workspace-specific roles; who approves accounts, role changes, and each
  assignment; whether one user may hold multiple roles.
- Which workspace structure and staff-assignment actions Workspace Owners may
  perform and which actions may be delegated to Workspace Administrators.
- Workspace invitation and join-request workflow, private discovery settings,
  membership revocation, and owner-transfer safeguards.
- Exact per-entity read, create, update, correction, and export permissions,
  assignment lifecycle, correction workflow, audit detail, retention, and
  archival behavior.
- Which learner details advisers may update and how corrections are reviewed.
- Teacher and Adviser attendance recording/correction authority; attendance
  status rules; whether initial entries need history events; and whether a
  section may have multiple attendance sessions per date and what identifies
  each session.
- Warning categories, evidence, review actions, referral routing, recipients,
  and notification/escalation rules.
- Which support-case fields are visible to each assigned staff role, whether
  a guidance assignment grants case access, and which records/notes must
  remain guidance-only.
- Whether to create a guardian-link entity, how identity and relationship are
  verified, which learner data is visible, and whether parent messaging or
  exports are permitted.
- Announcement authoring/editing authority, audience definitions, recipient
  authorization, read-receipt behavior, and whether edit history is needed.
- Permitted report fields, aggregate thresholds if any, export recipients,
  logging, and retention.
- What operational audit fields administrators may read, and how to prevent
  audit views from disclosing restricted record content.

This proposal does not assert deployment approval or legal compliance.
Authentication exists only as a configurable Supabase client integration;
authorization, database policies/RLS, and real-data access remain
unimplemented until their grants and policies are reviewed and tested.
